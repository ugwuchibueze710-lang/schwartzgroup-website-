Our Story

Transforming Homes Since 2009

Schwartz Group Home Improvement was founded on a simple principle: to provide homeowners with honest, reliable, and high-quality renovation services. What started as a small local operation in Evansville, Indiana, has grown into a trusted name in interior and exterior remodeling.

Over the past 15+ years, we have completed over 500 projects, ranging from complete kitchen and bathroom overhauls to full exterior makeovers. We believe that your home is your sanctuary, and our job is to enhance its beauty, functionality, and value without the stress of a typical construction project.
